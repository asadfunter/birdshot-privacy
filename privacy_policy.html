Privacy Policy for BirdShot

Last updated: June 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BirdShot ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the BirdShot mobile application ("App"). We are committed to keeping things simple and transparent — this app collects very little data.

Information We Collect

Device Notification Token (FCM Token)

When you install the App, Google Firebase automatically generates a device-specific token called a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token. This token is sent to our servers solely to deliver push notifications to your device, such as game alerts and challenge updates. This token does not identify you personally.

Auto-Generated Username

The App requests a randomly generated username from our servers. You do not enter or provide any personal information to receive this username — it is created entirely by our system. It is stored locally on your device and used only to identify your game session within the App.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your contacts or address book
  • Camera or microphone access
  • Any text or information you type
  • Any other personal information

How We Use the Information

  • FCM token — to send you optional push notifications (game alerts, challenges). You can disable notifications at any time from your device Settings.
  • Auto-generated username — to load your game session and associate notifications with your device.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy practices:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging) — used to deliver push notifications. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Game Content — the game is served from slingshot-runner.vercel.app. Your in-game activity is subject to that service's own privacy practices.

Data Retention

Your FCM token is stored on our servers only while the App is installed on your device. When you uninstall the App, Firebase automatically invalidates the token. We do not retain data beyond what is needed for notifications to function.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
